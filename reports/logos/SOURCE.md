# NBA team logos

SVGs converted from [React-NBA-Logos](https://github.com/ChrisKatsaras/React-NBA-Logos)
by Chris Katsaras, licensed **ISC**. The original components are JSX; they were
converted to standalone SVG (JSX attribute names normalized, `{' '}` whitespace
literals stripped) for use in the static report.

Filenames are lowercase team abbreviations matching the warehouse's current
franchise labels (e.g. `gsw.svg`). Team logos are trademarks of their respective
NBA franchises; used here for non-commercial analytics display.
